    How Irving Weight Loss works

    Five minutes online, a licensed provider review, and medication shipped to your door in the Irving area if treatment is right for you.

    1. Answer a few questions

    Tap Get Started anywhere on this site. The questionnaire asks about your goals, your health history, medications you take and what you have tried before. It takes about five minutes, costs nothing and does not commit you to anything.

    2. A licensed provider reviews your case

    A licensed Texas provider from the Oakwell MD medical team reads your answers. They may message you follow-up questions, ask for recent lab work, or schedule a short video visit when the situation calls for it. If treatment is appropriate, they recommend a specific medication, form and starting dose. If it is not, they tell you plainly and suggest what might help instead.

    3. Pricing, then your decision

    You see the plan and its price before you pay anything. Plans include the medication, provider oversight, messaging with the care team and dose adjustments. There is no insurance paperwork unless you choose to submit a receipt yourself.

    4. Shipped to your door

    Prescriptions are filled by licensed US pharmacies that meet state and federal standards, including 503A compounding rules for compounded medications. Packages are discreet and temperature-controlled where needed, and ship anywhere in the Irving area.

    5. Ongoing care

    GLP-1 treatment works best with a slow, steady step-up in dose. Your provider checks in on side effects, progress and goals, and adjusts the plan. You can message the care team any time with questions about missed doses, travel, side effects or plateaus.

    What we treat

    • GLP-1 weight loss: semaglutide and tirzepatide as weekly injections or daily dissolving tablets, compounded or brand name when appropriate
    • Peptide therapy for recovery, longevity, sleep and focus
    • Men's and women's sexual health
    • Hair growth, sleep and energy, wellness and longevity

    Compounded medications are prepared by licensed pharmacies to a provider's prescription and are not FDA-approved. A licensed provider decides whether any treatment is appropriate for you.

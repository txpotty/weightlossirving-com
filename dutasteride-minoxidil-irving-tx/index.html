    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Irving, TX

    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Irving

    Dutasteride blocks both type 1 and type 2 5-alpha reductase, the enzymes that turn testosterone into DHT. Because it hits both, it lowers DHT in the blood by more than 90 percent, compared with roughly 70 percent for finasteride, which blocks only type 2. Less DHT, less shrinking. Minoxidil adds a separate benefit by lengthening the growth phase. Together they handle both sides.

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride. A year of steady finasteride with photos is the usual bar before stepping up. Some men with aggressive early loss discuss it sooner. Women of childbearing potential cannot use it.

    Dosing and Schedule in Irving

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil goes on topically once or twice a day. Oral minoxidil is off-label.

    What to Expect Month by Month

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. The early shed is normal and usually fades. By months three to six, many men notice less loss and some thickening. In head-to-head studies, dutasteride 0.5 mg produced greater increases in hair count than finasteride 1 mg over six months, which is why it is the common step up. Give it a year.

    Side Effects of Dutasteride + Minoxidil in Irving

    Dutasteride can lower libido, affect erections or reduce ejaculate volume in a small share of men, similar to finasteride. Breast tenderness or enlargement is rare, and because the drug lingers for months, side effects may take longer to fade after stopping than they would with finasteride. Report low mood promptly. Scalp itch is the usual topical issue. The oral form sometimes causes fluid retention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your provider asks about liver health, mood history, sexual health and current medications. Dutasteride lowers PSA by about half, so any doctor screening your prostate needs to know you take it and will usually double the reading to interpret it. No blood donation during use or for six months after. Pregnant partners should not touch leaking capsules.

    How It Compares to Other Options in Irving

Dutasteride + minoxidil is stronger but off-label for hair in the US, and its long half-life makes it slower to wash out if side effects show up. Minoxidil + GHK-Cu is a topical-only choice.

    How the Antiparasitic Protocol Works in Carrollton

    The protocol starts with your symptoms and possible exposures, not with a pill. The provider asks which parasites make sense given where you have traveled, what pets you have and whether people in your household have the same complaints. Then comes a testing decision. You may be asked to get a stool ova and parasite test or a specific antigen test before treatment. Treatment uses prescription anti-parasitics with established uses, and a follow-up confirms symptoms are gone.

    Who This Protocol Is For

    Adults with symptoms or exposures that raise a fair concern for a parasite are the right fit. Nighttime anal itching in a household with kids, which usually means pinworm, is the most common reason people start. Recent travel to regions where intestinal worms are common is another, as is a positive stool test from a previous clinic. It is not a detox. It has no role in weight loss either.

    Dosing and Schedule in Carrollton

    The schedule depends on the drug and the parasite. For pinworm, a single dose of mebendazole 100 mg or albendazole 400 mg is typical, with a second dose two weeks later for newly hatched worms. Ivermectin depends on your body weight. It is usually given as one dose or a short course of one to two days. Your provider writes out exact dates for every dose.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Most courses take one to three days, sometimes with a repeat dose two weeks later. Some people feel a little queasy or tired for a day after the pills. With pinworm, the itching usually eases within a few days, and the remaining irritation can take another week to settle. Week two is the repeat dose. Around week four, your provider may ask for a symptom update or a repeat test.

    Side Effects and Precautions in Carrollton

    Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhea and headache are the side effects people mention most often. With ivermectin, a temporary rash or itch sometimes appears as the parasites die. Because mebendazole and albendazole stay mostly in the gut at standard doses, short courses rarely cause much trouble. Pregnancy changes things. Some anti-parasitics are avoided in pregnancy, so tell your provider if there is any chance.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks when symptoms started, where you have traveled in the past year and whether pets or family members are involved. Upload any previous stool test results. Providers also screen for pregnancy, breastfeeding, liver disease and current medications that could interact, such as warfarin with ivermectin. Bloody stools, high fever or severe belly pain lead to an in-person referral. Some people are asked to finish testing before any medication is prescribed.

    How the Protocol Compares to Over-the-Counter Cleanses in Carrollton

    Herbal parasite cleanses sold online are not regulated as medications. Doses differ between brands and even between batches, and few have been studied against any particular parasite. Prescription anti-parasitics have known doses. Ivermectin and mebendazole, both offered individually through Irving Weight Loss, each work best against particular parasites. Your provider picks one drug rather than layering several products.

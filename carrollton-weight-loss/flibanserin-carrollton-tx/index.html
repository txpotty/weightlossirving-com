    How Flibanserin Treatment Works for Women in Carrollton, TX

    How Flibanserin Works in Carrollton

    Flibanserin acts on serotonin receptors in the brain, not on blood flow and not on hormones. By turning down some serotonin activity, it is thought to give dopamine and norepinephrine, the chemicals linked with interest and motivation, more room to work. It is not a hormone. Viagra works on blood flow, so the two are not comparable. Any change comes gradually, after several weeks of taking it every night.

    Who Flibanserin Is Meant For

    The FDA approval covers premenopausal women with acquired, generalized HSDD. That means interest was fine before, it has faded across the board, and the change is causing you distress. Not every dip qualifies. If the main cause is an antidepressant, a thyroid problem or conflict at home, flibanserin is not the first answer. Postmenopausal women are not part of the labeled population.

    Dosing and the Nightly Schedule in Carrollton

    The dose is one 100 mg tablet, once a day, at bedtime. Always at bedtime. Dizziness and sleepiness peak in the hours right after a dose, so taking it before sleep means most of that happens while you are already asleep. Unlike some options, it is not taken on demand. Missed a dose? Skip it, and do not double up the next night.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one is mostly about side effects, usually mild sleepiness or dizziness that eases. That part usually passes. By weeks four to six, women who respond often notice they think about sex more and mind the low desire less. In the clinical trials, the average gain over placebo was modest, about one additional satisfying sexual event a month, along with less distress. At eight weeks the label says to stop if there is no improvement, and your provider will ask you directly.

    Side Effects of Flibanserin in Carrollton

    The most common side effects are dizziness, sleepiness, nausea and fatigue. Dry mouth and trouble sleeping show up too. The serious risk is a sharp drop in blood pressure that can cause fainting, and it is more likely after alcohol or with drugs that raise flibanserin levels. Do not drive or run machinery for at least six hours after a dose. If you ever feel faint, lie down and contact your provider.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with your full medication list. Flibanserin cannot be combined with moderate or strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, which include fluconazole, ketoconazole, clarithromycin, several HIV medicines and large amounts of grapefruit juice. Liver impairment of any degree rules it out. Expect questions about past fainting, blood pressure, depression, pregnancy plans and weekly drinks.

    Flibanserin Compared With PT-141 and Other Options in Carrollton

    Flibanserin and bremelanotide, sold as Vyleesi and often called PT-141, are the two FDA-approved medicines for HSDD before menopause. The difference is mostly routine: flibanserin is a nightly tablet, while PT-141 is a small injection used about 45 minutes before sex. PT-141 has no daily alcohol timing, which matters to some women. Oxytocin therapy is compounded and used off-label, and it is not FDA-approved for desire. Your provider explains which fits.

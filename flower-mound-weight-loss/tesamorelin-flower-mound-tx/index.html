    How Tesamorelin Treatment Works in Flower Mound, TX

    How Tesamorelin Works in Flower Mound

    Tesamorelin is a stabilized version of growth hormone releasing hormone. It signals the pituitary to release growth hormone in pulses, which raises IGF-1 and pushes the body to break down fat, especially the visceral fat stored around the liver and intestines. Feedback controls stay active. The brand is Egrifta SV, and compounded versions also exist.

    Who Tesamorelin Is Usually For

    The approved use is narrow, excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV who have lipodystrophy. Off-label, it may be considered for adults whose waist circumference and metabolic labs point to visceral fat as the main issue, sometimes with fatty liver. It is not a general weight loss drug. Pregnancy, active cancer and prior pituitary surgery or head radiation rule it out.

    Tesamorelin Dosing and Schedule in Flower Mound

    Once a day, into the belly. Egrifta SV uses a fixed 1.4 milligram daily dose given under the skin of the abdomen, while compounded protocols can differ and your provider explains the exact amount. Moving the injection site each day reduces irritation. A fixed time each day makes missed doses less likely.

    What to Expect Over Six Months

    The first month is mostly habit building. Clinical studies judged results at 26 weeks, when visceral fat had fallen by roughly 15 to 18 percent on average. Some people see the waistband loosen around months two and three. If there is little change at 26 weeks, the label and your provider both point toward stopping.

    Tesamorelin Side Effects in Flower Mound

    Joint pain is common. Injection site redness, itching and pain, muscle aches and pain in the arms or legs are the other frequent ones. Swollen hands or ankles and tingling fingers also happen, and because the drug can push glucose up, people with prediabetes or diabetes need closer monitoring. Rash or hives need prompt attention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Before prescribing, a provider reviews your history, medications and labs. Baseline testing usually includes A1C or fasting glucose, IGF-1 and a metabolic panel, and people with diabetes may be asked about recent eye exams because of retinopathy risk. You will be asked about cancer, pituitary conditions and pregnancy plans. Lab reports can be uploaded as photos.

    Tesamorelin Compared to Related Options in Flower Mound

    Sermorelin is the closest relative. Tesamorelin and sermorelin act the same way, yet tesamorelin lasts longer and has real clinical evidence for deep belly fat. The CJC-1295 and ipamorelin pairing works on a similar axis without any FDA approval. GLP-1 drugs like semaglutide and tirzepatide lower total body weight through appetite, which is a different job.

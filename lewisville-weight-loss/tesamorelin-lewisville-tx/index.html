    How Tesamorelin Treatment Works in Lewisville, TX

    How Tesamorelin Works in Lewisville

    Tesamorelin is a stabilized version of growth hormone releasing hormone. After injection it prompts pulsed growth hormone release, IGF-1 rises, and fat breakdown increases, with the biggest effect on visceral fat deep inside the abdomen. Feedback controls stay active. The brand is Egrifta SV, and compounded versions also exist.

    Who Tesamorelin Is Usually For

    The approved use is narrow, excess abdominal fat in adults with HIV who have lipodystrophy. Off-label, it may be considered for adults whose waist circumference and metabolic labs point to visceral fat as the main issue, sometimes with fatty liver. It is not a general weight loss drug. Pregnancy, active cancer and prior pituitary surgery or head radiation rule it out.

    Tesamorelin Dosing and Schedule in Lewisville

    Once a day, into the belly. Egrifta SV uses a fixed 1.4 milligram daily dose given under the skin of the abdomen, while compounded protocols can differ and your provider explains the exact amount. Rotate injection spots around the belly to cut down on redness. Pick the same time daily so it becomes routine.

    What to Expect Over Six Months

    The first month is mostly habit building. In the approval trials, visceral fat was measured at 26 weeks and had dropped about 15 to 18 percent on average, so six months is the real checkpoint. A looser waistband sometimes shows up by month three. With no meaningful change by six months, providers usually recommend stopping.

    Tesamorelin Side Effects in Lewisville

    Joint pain is common. Other common complaints are redness or itching at the site, sore muscles and aching arms or legs. Swollen hands or ankles and tingling fingers also happen, and because the drug can push glucose up, people with prediabetes or diabetes need closer monitoring. Rash or hives need prompt attention.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history, medications and labs. Expect A1C or fasting glucose, IGF-1 and a metabolic panel, and if you have diabetes, your provider may ask when your last dilated eye exam was. You will be asked about cancer, pituitary conditions and pregnancy plans. Lab reports can be uploaded as photos.

    Tesamorelin Compared to Related Options in Lewisville

    Sermorelin is the closest relative. Tesamorelin and sermorelin act the same way, yet tesamorelin lasts longer and has real clinical evidence for deep belly fat. CJC-1295 with ipamorelin also stimulates growth hormone but has no FDA-approved use. GLP-1 drugs like semaglutide and tirzepatide lower total body weight through appetite, which is a different job.
